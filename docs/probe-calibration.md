# Probe and anchor calibration

Device → **Anchors** offers **Auto calibrate Anchor 1** on the Z1 and **Calibrate 3D probe diameter**. Both are illustrated guides that prepare separate probing plates. Opening a guide, editing settings and preparing a plate send no machine commands. Review and start motion with **Run** on Job; normal homing, tool-change, probe and job checks remain in force. Do not confuse the wired Z-only laser probe with the selected XYZ touch probe.

Setup confirmations apply to the probe entry and connected device currently shown. Changing the probe, its library data or the device connection requires confirmation again; a missing selected probe is never silently replaced. Moving between guide steps also clears confirmation. Anchor calibration additionally requires confirmation again after the device's anchor configuration changes; diameter calibration does so after settings or readings change.

## Anchor 1

Use the supplied L-bracket pinned and secured in its normal Anchor 1 position, with clean top and inner faces. Select the installed bracket (thick, 15 mm tall, or thin, 5 mm tall) and the physical 3D probe's library entry with its effective ball diameter. The guide adds the selected bracket to its calibration plate at the normal pinned position, replacing any inherited bracket variants on that plate. The bracket's inner corner and the plate's work XY origin are at Anchor 1 at the front left; the preview uses its actual modeled arms rather than inventing a corner at the current probe position.

Home the machine and test the probe signal. On Run, normal tool measurement is followed by a machine-clearance retract and anchored XY travel to **Anchor 1 + X−5, Y−5 mm**, over the solid bracket top. Manual jogging to the start is not required. There is no work-Z descent before probing: it searches down from clearance for the top. The job uses the existing front-left inside-corner routine: two top touches, then two contacts on each inner wall, with 10 mm X/Y distances and a 2 mm side depth below the top. It changes the active work X/Y/Z origin. This is fine calibration: the existing stored anchor must be close enough that the start is actually over the bracket, and the operator must verify the entire route and installed reference. It cannot discover a completely misplaced bracket or recover unknown machine coordinates.

The completed result offers **Save as Anchor 1**, locked to that target. Review old/new machine coordinates before **Write anchors**. The write uses the existing acknowledged, read-back-verified anchor writer; it preserves the other anchors' offsets, so they move with Anchor 1. A job from another device, a changed anchor configuration, an edited corner strategy or an unfinished job cannot save this calibration. Older drafts that used the current probe position, or calibration plates whose reference fixture, stored anchor positions or anchored start changed after preparation, block Run and NC export: recreate them with the guide. Rereading identical positions is allowed. Reset and re-home after saving so firmware-owned tool-change and tool-setter moves use the new configuration. This guide does not reset, home or write automatically. It does not measure probe diameter or spindle alignment.

This workflow uses the app's existing inside-corner cycle and is informed by [GabrielMaker's Anchor 1 calibration video description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4rQWrt5rDqo). Physical validation of the new UI flow remains an operator task.

## Effective probe diameter

The four-step guide implements the known-width Boss-X method supplied with [GabrielMaker's diameter calibration video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rj9dhoIGyUQ) and the user-supplied **3D Touch Probe Diameter Calculator - GabrielMaker.html**. The calculator's scripts are not embedded or executed. Pasted commands are parsed as data and are never sent to the machine.

1. Secure a reference with a known width along machine X, clear of the machine edges. The supplied bracket is nominally 15.000 mm wide; use a measured reference value when available. Do not leave it at its normal Anchor 1 position: the probe needs space outside both sides. The measured side faces are perpendicular to X.
2. Select the physical XYZ probe, fit and cable it, home and test the probe signal. Start over the reference center, above its top. Defaults are 10 mm X offset, 2.5 mm side depth and initial D2.000. The offset must exceed half the width plus ball radius plus 1 mm. Check the entire route, including screws and clamps.
3. Prepare reading 1, Run it on Job and reopen the guide **before Dismiss**. **Collect completed reading** uses the frozen run, its originating device, its probe and the attributed complete Boss-X contacts. Each successive job uses the previous reading's effective diameter, rounded to three decimals. The guide uses a separate calibration probe library entry; it leaves the original entry unchanged until Save. Reposition over the same reference center before each Run. Closing the guide retains its draft while the app is open; it is not an automatic background job.
4. Review all three readings, confirm that they are separate physical cycles on the selected probe/reference, and save the final effective diameter to the original probe. Save updates its diameter and appends calibration notes as an undoable library edit. Future generated probing NC takes that library diameter; already exported programs and a frozen Run do not change. Export the JSON report to retain raw contacts, inputs, provenance and results before closing the app. Saved diameter and notes persist through the normal tool-library persistence.

Manual mode accepts one complete MDI log for each reading, including a single **M480.10 D… X… Y0 Z…** command and exactly six successful PRB reports after it: two top, two left and two right. Earlier TLO contacts are ignored because they precede that command. Ambiguous, mixed, failed and incomplete routines are rejected. Native collection also rejects reused run IDs, edited parameters and different devices. Editing a simulator reading does not remove its practice-only status. Pasted logs have operator-supplied provenance, which the app cannot verify.

The calculation follows the supplied calculator: left mean = average of both left contacts; right mean = average of both right contacts; contact span = right mean − left mean; effective diameter = span − known width. The result is the average of three effective diameters, rounded to 0.001 mm. For example 16.9735 − 15.0000 = 1.9735 mm, which rounds to D1.974. This differs intentionally from normal feature measurement, which uses the slower contact on each side.

Application checks require effective diameter 0.5–10 mm, side Y and Z spread at most 0.01 mm, at most 0.1 mm between the two X contacts on each side, and a final three-reading diameter range at most 0.02 mm. These are screening thresholds, not accuracy guarantees. Simulator readings can exercise the guide but cannot be saved as physical calibration. This is scalar X-derived compensation; Makera also applies that diameter to Y, whose response is not measured here. Keep the same stylus, mounting orientation and probing conditions; recalibrate after changing them. Effective diameter is compensation, not a claim that the physical ball changed size.

## Manual verification

- In simulation, complete each prepared reading and collect it before Dismiss. Confirm that the next D uses the preceding result, a run cannot be collected twice, another plate/device cannot supply it, and simulated readings cannot be saved to the original probe.
- Paste malformed, failed, truncated, multi-routine and Boss-XY output; each must show an error. Use three separate valid logs to review the average/range and export the report. A change to the original library probe must block saving the old proposal.
- For Anchor 1, select each bracket variant and inspect its front-left placement and the generated inside-corner route. The program must retract to machine clearance and travel to the stored anchor minus 5 mm in both axes before M480.8; changing the current work zero or probe position must not relocate that start. Only a complete, attributed result exposes the dedicated saving flow. Removing/disabling/moving the bracket or using an old current-position calibration draft must block generation. Changing stored anchors or running another job before saving must block that old calibration. Inspect the proposed X/Y changes and offset-preserving movement of the other anchors before writing.
- Physical probing requires operator verification of the installed reference, probe signal and travel. Static build checks do not validate real clearance, probe repeatability or calibration accuracy.
